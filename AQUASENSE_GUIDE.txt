# AquaSense360: Fish Tracker & System Notifications Guide

## 1. How the Fish Tracker Works

The fish tracker uses a combination of Computer Vision (YOLO) and a Centroid Tracking algorithm to monitor individual fish in real-time.

### Step 1: Detection (YOLO)
The system uses the YOLO (You Only Look Once) model to detect fish in the video stream. For every frame, it identifies the bounding box (location) of each fish.

### Step 2: Tracking (Centroid Tracking)
The `CentroidTracker` class (`fish_tracker.py`) manages individual fish identities:
- **Registration**: When a new fish appears, it is assigned a unique ID.
- **Centroid Calculation**: It calculates the center point (centroid) of each detected fish.
- **Tracking**: It matches centroids in the current frame to the closest centroids in the previous frame to ensure "Fish #1" stays "Fish #1" as it swims.
- **Persistence**: If a fish disappears briefly (e.g., swims behind a plant), the system remembers it for up to 15 frames before deregistering it.

### Step 3: Behavior Analysis
The system analyzes the movement of each tracked fish:
- **Speed**: Calculated as pixels moved per second.
- **Erraticness**: It measures the angle of direction changes. If a fish turns sharply and frequently, it marks the movement as erratic.
- **Health Labels**:
  - **NORMAL**: Smooth swimming at a healthy speed.
  - **ABNORMAL**: Speed is too low (< 10 px/s) or movement is highly erratic (> 60° direction changes). This can indicate stress or illness.

## 2. System Notifications (Pop-ups)

The system displays toast notifications (top-right pop-ups) for critical events. These are categorized by severity.

### 🔴 Danger / Critical (Red)
These require immediate attention:
- **Critical Sensors**:
  - **Temperature**: Extremely low (< 19.2°C) or Extremely high (> 36°C).
  - **pH Level**: Highly acidic (< 5.2) or Highly alkaline (> 10.2).
  - **Water Level**: Critically low (Distance > 18cm).
  - **CO2/TDS**: Values far outside safe ranges.
- **AI Alerts**:
  - **Poor Water Quality**: AI predicts "Poor" water conditions.
  - **Fish Disease Detected**: YOLO detects signs of disease (e.g., White Spot, Fin Rot).
  - **Dangerous Gas**: Dangerous gas levels detected in the tank area.

### 🟡 Warning (Orange)
Important alerts that aren't yet critical:
- **Sensor Thresholds**: Values slightly outside the optimal range (e.g., pH 8.6 or Temperature 23°C).
- **Moderate Water Quality**: AI predicts water quality is becoming "Moderate".
- **Low Water Level**: Water level is getting low (Distance > 15cm).

### 🔵 Info / System (Blue)
General updates:
- **Motion Detection (PIR)**: "Motion detected near fish tank" (triggered by the infrared sensor).
- **Device Status**: Notifications when the ESP32 or Laptop (ML Service) goes online/offline.

## 3. Notification Bell Visual Indicators

The Notification Bell in the top navigation bar uses several visual cues to alert you:

### 🔴 The Red Badge
- **Unread Count**: A red circle appears on the bell icon showing the number of new alerts.
- **"9+" Display**: If there are more than 9 unread alerts, it simply shows "9+".
- **Pulse Animation**: The red badge pulses (grows and shrinks slightly) every 2 seconds to grab your attention when there are unread critical messages.

### 📋 The Dropdown List
When you click the bell, a list appears with these indicators:
- **Left Border Color**: Each alert has a colored vertical bar on the left (Red for Danger, Orange for Warning, Blue for Info).
- **Icons**:
  - 🚨 **Emergency**: Critical sensor or disease alerts.
  - ⚠️ **Warning**: Out-of-range sensor values.
  - 👁️ **Eye**: Motion (PIR) detections.
  - ℹ️ **Blue Info**: General system updates.
- **Relative Time**: Shows how long ago the alert happened (e.g., "2 minutes ago").

### 🧹 Cleaning Up
- **Unread Reset**: Clicking the bell to open the list automatically clears the unread count (the red badge disappears).
- **Clear All**: You can use the "Clear All" button in the dropdown to empty the list.
